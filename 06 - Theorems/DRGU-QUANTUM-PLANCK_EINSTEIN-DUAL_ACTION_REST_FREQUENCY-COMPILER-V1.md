# DRGU QUANTUM PLANCK_EINSTEIN DUAL_ACTION_REST_FREQUENCY COMPILER

## Equations

### 1. Invariant rest energy

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E01`

**Formula:** `E_{\mathrm{rest}}=mc^2`

**Interpretation:** Invariant mass m maps to rest energy through c squared.

**Status:** canonical

### 2. Cyclic Planck form

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E02`

**Formula:** `E_{\mathrm{rest}}=h\nu_C`

**Interpretation:** Full-cycle frequency nu_C prices the same rest energy with h.

**Status:** canonical

### 3. Angular Planck form

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E03`

**Formula:** `E_{\mathrm{rest}}=\hbar\omega_C`

**Interpretation:** Angular frequency omega_C prices the same rest energy with hbar.

**Status:** canonical

### 4. Planck ratio

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E04`

**Formula:** `h=\tau\hbar`

**Interpretation:** h and hbar differ by the exact full-cycle factor tau=2pi.

**Status:** canonical

### 5. Frequency ratio

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E05`

**Formula:** `\omega_C=\tau\nu_C`

**Interpretation:** Angular and cyclic frequencies differ by tau.

**Status:** canonical

### 6. Dual-action equivalence

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E06`

**Formula:** `h\nu_C=\hbar\omega_C`

**Interpretation:** The two Planck forms are one energy in two normalizations.

**Status:** canonical

### 7. Compton cyclic frequency

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E07`

### 8. Compton angular frequency

### 9. Photon dual-action energy

**Key:** `EQ.DRGU.PLANCK_EINSTEIN.DUAL_ACTION.E09`

**Formula:** `E_\gamma=h\nu_\gamma=\hbar\omega_\gamma`

**Interpretation:** For a photon, cyclic and angular frequency forms describe the same photon energy.

**Status:** canonical
